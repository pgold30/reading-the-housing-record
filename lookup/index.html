    Method & underlying papers

    This lookup aggregates dataset release v1.2.0 (Data Note v1.2.2; 729,047 raw rows, 503,706 residential sales at $10,000 or more across 2016–2025). Major condo buildings require at least 25 qualifying condo sales and exclude NYC placeholder BINs ending in 000000. Because condo deeds link to individual unit lots (1001–6999), they are grouped at the building level via BIN (bin-*) so unit transfers do not split a building apart.
