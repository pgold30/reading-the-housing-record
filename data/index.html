NYC sales linked to deeds and mortgages

New York City residential sales, 2016–2025, with deed links and nearby recorded mortgage matches for eligible houses and condominiums in the four ACRIS boroughs

Why these records need linking

New York City publishes its property sales and its recorded deeds and mortgages as separate open datasets, and the two do not join directly. The Department of Finance sales file has the price and date but no document number. ACRIS, the city register, has the deeds and mortgages but not the sales. Condominiums are the hardest case: the sales file usually identifies a unit by the building's billing lot, while ACRIS records its deed and mortgage under the unit's own lot, so a join on the parcel number misses most of them.

This dataset links the records using fixed, documented rules. The studies on this site and other researchers can reuse the same matches. The public files contain no party names.

In numbers

  • 729,047house, condominium and co-operative sales, 2016–2025, all five boroughs
  • 97.2%eligible house sales with a deed link in release 1.2.0

The release links 183,912 eligible house sales and 121,263 eligible condominium sales to deeds. Condominium coverage is 96.0%. A link identifies a candidate document under the stated rules; it does not verify the transaction or the purpose of a mortgage.

Files in the deposited 1.2.0 dataset

Three tables that share keys: sales joins to links on sale_id, and links joins to deed_flags on deed_document_id. A blank cell means not applicable or not measured, never zero.

How the link works

  • Deed. A deed matches a sale when its amount is within 0.5% of the price and its date within 45 days. Houses are matched on their own parcel. Condominiums are matched within the block among unit lots, which recovers the unit; it agrees with the sales file's own unit lot in 99.6% of single-parcel links. If several units qualify, the separate unit-lot field can resolve the choice; bulk purchases that fail the documented guards remain unlinked. If several deeds qualify on the same parcel, the lowest document identifier is kept, which picks one without showing which is correct; this happens for 946 of the 305,175 links.
  • Mortgage. A sale receives a recorded-mortgage flag if a mortgage is recorded on its parcel (for condominiums, the recovered unit) from 15 days before to 30, 90 or 180 days after the sale. A recorded mortgage is not verified purchase finance.
  • Flags. Computed from the ACRIS party records of each deed, with the papers' own rules. Party names were used to compute flags and are excluded from the public release. The flags are a fixed snapshot.

What has been checked

The preserved extracts rebuild the sales and link tables. Computational checks cover join keys, document types, collateral parcels, amount tolerances and date windows. The included example also checks financing missingness and join completeness. These checks establish consistency with the source tables and the stated rules; they do not measure the accuracy of accepted or missed links, or establish purchase purpose.

Limits

  • A financed flag of 0 means the search found no qualifying recorded mortgage in the window. It is not proof of a cash purchase.
  • Co-operative and Staten Island sales are listed but never linked. ACRIS real-property mortgages do not measure financing secured by co-op shares, and Staten Island deeds are outside ACRIS.
  • Condominium coverage remains lower at high prices: 71.6% of sales at $6 million or more link to a deed. Comparisons based on linked sales describe that selected subset.
  • The company, surname, address and deed-event flags are screens built from names. Existing review agreement does not establish independently verified population accuracy. The party records are not deposited, so these flags are supplied as a fixed snapshot.

Condominium coverage

Version 1.2.0 corrects the unit-lot limit in my code. Coverage is 94.7% in Manhattan, 98.8% in the Bronx, 97.7% in Brooklyn and 96.1% in Queens. The former Bronx gap was caused by that code restriction, not evidence of missing public records. Read the correction.

Coverage measures how many records link. Accuracy requires checking the links against source documents.
