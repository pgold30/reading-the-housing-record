Form TC107 step-by-step guide: how NYC pied-à-terre surcharge appeals work in 2027

New York City’s Department of Finance (DOF) administrative exemption deadline passed on October 13, 2026. For owners who missed that window or face a steep whole-parcel surcharge cliff, NYC Tax Commission Form TC107 is the paper filing route due March 1, 2027 (condos & co-ops) or March 15, 2027 (1–3 family houses).

Under NY Tax Law §§ 1350–1353, crossing a threshold triggers the rate on the parcel’s entire DOF market value, not just the dollars above the line. A Class 2 condo jumping from $999,999 to $1,000,000 moves immediately from $0 to $40,000/year before exemptions.

Step 1: Verify primary-residence & statutory exemptions ($0 if exempt)

Before spending money on valuation arguments, verify whether the property owes $0 regardless of its DOF value. Under Tax Law §§ 1350–1353, primary residences, qualifying leased units (rented to a primary-residence tenant), qualifying family-occupied residences, and covered exemptions are not subject to the surcharge.

Because DOF’s October 13, 2026 administrative exemption deadline passed and initial November 14 tax statements already billed roughly 8,000 properties, Tax Commission guidance notes that owners who missed the DOF window can use Form TC107 paired with a valuation challenge before the March deadlines. Entity- and trust-owned units carry strict documentation rules (income tax returns, driver’s licenses, utility records, leases, and trust/entity ownership proof).

Step 2: Match the right valuation standard for your tax class

Owners frequently make the mistake of bringing recent apartment sale comparables into a Class 2 condo valuation review:

  • Tax Class 2 Condos & Co-ops (RPTL § 581 Rental Comps): During Phase 1 (July 2026–June 2028), New York State Real Property Tax Law § 581 requires NYC DOF to value condominiums and co-ops as if they were comparable rental buildings, using net rental income estimates shown on the building’s Notice of Property Value (NOPV) — not individual apartment sale prices. Co-op buildings are assessed as one undivided parcel, while condo unit owners appeal separately by unit BBL (or through authorized counsel/board filings).
  • Tax Class 1 Houses (Sales Comparables): One- to three-family houses are assessed near market value (median 105.5% of sale price in our empirical match), so market-value challenges rely directly on comparable arm’s-length sales.

Step 3: Paper-only execution (no email, no fax)

The NYC Tax Commission enforces strict procedural instructions for Form TC107:

  • Physical paper submission only: Emailing or faxing Form TC107 or its exhibits is not permitted. Forms require a physical wet signature.
  • Where to deliver: Submit in person or by mail to the NYC Tax Commission, 1 Centre Street, Room 2400, New York, NY 10007 (or designated DOF Business Centers). Keep a complete photocopy of every signed page and attachment.
  • Separate application per unit: Condos and co-ops require a separate Form TC107 package per individual unit.
  • One surcharge appeal per year & complete answers: Leaving required items blank or omitting required residency/authority proof (such as an agent’s power of attorney) can result in denial without a reduction.

Step 4: Pull your parcel’s baseline data before preparing

Whether you prepare your own filing or hand the numbers to a New York tax-certiorari attorney, start with the exact DOF roll numbers (finmkttot, year-over-year change, prior DOF protest code, and building peer disparity) rather than guessing.
