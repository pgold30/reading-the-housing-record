Plain-English Filing Guide · March 1 & March 15, 2027

NYC pied-à-terre surcharge appeals and Form TC107

Check the deadlines, application requirements and supporting evidence for an NYC Tax Commission surcharge appeal.

What Form TC107 covers

The form covers the non-primary residence surcharge. The grounds are Excessive or Unlawful. On an excessive-value claim, the applicant states the claimed Full Value under oath. The instructions state: “The TC107 cannot be used to protest the assessed value of your Property.” See the Tax Commission forms and instructions for the grounds and evidence it accepts.

Check the deadline for your tax class

  • March 1, 2027 for Class 2 market-value challenges, including covered condos and co-ops.
  • March 15, 2027 for Class 1 market-value challenges.

These dates also apply when asking the Tax Commission to review the initial Department of Finance (DOF) residency determination and the market value together for 2026–27 and 2027–28. If you first challenged residency with DOF, the residency appeal can be filed by the applicable March date or within 30 days of the final DOF notice, whichever is later. The March deadline still applies to market value.

Check the notice and the evidence

The surcharge notice gives the city’s value and residency determination. The primary-residence test uses the taxable status date before the applicable fiscal year (January 5, 2026 for 2026–27). It can be met by a covered owner, their spouse, child, sibling, parent, grandparent or grandchild, or a tenant or subtenant on a bona fide arm’s-length lease of at least one year. Property below the entry threshold or outside the covered classes incurs no surcharge. Section 1351(g)(1) excludes property still awaiting a required certificate of occupancy. Section 1351(g)(2) excludes units subject to an offering plan when the filer has yet to sell or transfer an economic interest in the unit. Section 1354(a) provides that “any abatement, credit or exemption authorized by law shall not apply to such surcharge”. See NY Tax Law Article 30-C (§§ 1350–1356), enacted as Part HH of Chapter 59 of the Laws of 2026 and the form instructions for the definitions and proof.

For a Class 2 Phase 1 value challenge, the instructions describe full value in terms of the income of comparable rental properties. Recorded condo sale prices help explain the difference between the city’s value and a sale price. See the rental-comparables guide.

File the application on paper

  • File the paper application with an original signature. Mail it to the Tax Commission at 1 Centre Street, Room 2400, New York, NY 10007 or file in person there or at a designated DOF business center. The application must arrive by the deadline. Include Form TC10 and a stamped, self-addressed envelope if filing by mail.
  • Use a separate application for each condo or co-op unit. Appeal combined apartments as one residence. A co-op corporation may file for a unit owner. The Tax Commission accepts one surcharge appeal per property per year.
  • An agent signing under a power of attorney must attach the authorization and Form TC244. Use Form TC155 to change the designated representative.
  • Supporting documents may be emailed to TCSurchargeAppeal@oata.nyc.gov. Material submitted after the application requires Form TC159 and must arrive within 30 days of the filing deadline.
