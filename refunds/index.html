Refund policy

When you can get your money back for a public-record assessment report.

Full refund within 14 days

Write to me within 14 days of purchase and you get a full refund if:

  • the report shows that your parcel is out of scope (not a condominium unit, co-op or one- to three-family house covered by the surcharge);
  • the report is for a different parcel from the one you entered, because of an error on my side;
  • you were charged twice for the same parcel; or
  • the download failed and the recovery page did not work.

I also consider other requests made within 14 days.

How to ask

Email loschi.pablo@gmail.com with the parcel number (BBL) and the email you used at checkout, or the receipt number from Stripe’s email. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

See also the terms of use and the privacy page.
