NYC pied-à-terre tax deadlines: January 1 bills and Form TC107

All key statutory dates for the FY2027 and FY2028 NYC non-primary residence surcharge, including the March 1 Class 2 and March 15 Class 1 Form TC107 deadlines.

Mark the date for your tax class

  • March 1, 2027 for Tax Class 2 (condominiums and cooperatives valued at $1 million or more on the city roll).
  • March 15, 2027 for Tax Class 1 (one-, two-, and three-family homes valued at $5 million or more on the city roll).

The Tax Commission lists these deadlines for 2026–27 and 2027–28 market-value challenges and for applications that also challenge the initial residency determination. Read the official TC107 instructions for the deadline that applies to your notice, and use the free property checker to verify your unit's FY2027 city market value and tax class.

If you first challenged residency with DOF or missed October 13

The city’s 2026–27 direct administrative exemption application deadline with DOF was October 13, 2026. For an appeal of DOF’s final residency decision to the Tax Commission, the deadline is the later of the applicable March date or 30 days from the date of the final determination notice. Market-value appeals still need to meet March 1 (Class 2) or March 15 (Class 1).

Send the signed application on paper

The Tax Commission must receive Form TC107 on paper with an original signature by the applicable deadline (1 Centre Street, Room 2400, New York, NY 10007, or a designated DOF business center). Supporting documents may be emailed, and additional material sent after the application requires Form TC159 within 30 days of the filing deadline. See the Form TC107 step-by-step guide or review a Sample Assessment Report PDF ($79).
