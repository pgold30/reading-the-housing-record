About these records

The explorer uses the frozen NYC linked dataset, with sales dated 2016–2025 and source files retrieved on 26 September 2026. Of 729,047 input rows, 515,590 have a positive recorded price; zero-price rows are excluded here. It covers 1–3 family houses, condominiums and co-operative apartments. Other property types are outside this release. Each row is a sales-file entry; rows are not deduplicated by deed.

The published data version is 1.0; documentation is 1.0.2. Filters pool records before calculating the median. Price bands include their lower boundary and exclude their upper boundary. All positive prices includes low and nominal amounts. Coverage measures how many records linked. Accuracy requires checking those links against the source documents.
