23 November 2026 · New working paper & replication archive

Rental Proxies on a Million-Dollar Notch v1.0

Published Rental Proxies on a Million-Dollar Notch: Valuation Distortion, Horizontal Inequity, and Phase-2 Readiness in New York City's Pied-à-Terre Tax (10.5281/zenodo.24484929). Linking 968,294 FY2026-27 DOF parcels to 26,531 clean condo sales and 36,352 house sales, the study documents a 0.2344 condominium assessment-to-sale ratio (vs. 1.0549 for houses), effective surcharge inversion above $5 million (0.696% in $5M–$7.5M down to 0.440% at $20M+), 2SLS valuation elasticities of −0.1419 (repeat sales, N = 10,232) and −0.1428 (leave-one-out peer IV), 77.20% building-level variance splitting $4M–$6M condos between $0 (61.50%) and $40,000+ (38.50%), and 31.30% non-arm's-length entries among raw $5M+ condo sales ahead of Phase 2.

18 November 2026 · Research correction

Who Gets the House? corrects the $1 million comparison

Version 5.1 groups repeat-sale pairs by the geometric mean of both prices, with a check using the first sale price. The earlier split used the unfinanced sale price, which mechanically affected the measured price change. Under either revised definition, the confidence intervals for the upper band include zero. The overall estimates of 9.25 log points and 4.87 after the surname screen still hold.
