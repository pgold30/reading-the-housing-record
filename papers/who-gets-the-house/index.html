Who Gets the House?

Related-Party Transfers and the Cash–Mortgage Price Gap in New York City

By Pablo LoschiPublic version 3.1Independent document review pending

The question

Buyers who use a mortgage and buyers recorded as paying cash can appear to pay different prices for the same home. Is that contrast about financing, or about which people and transactions appear in each group?

The paper follows repeat sales of the same New York City houses and condominiums in 2016–2025, linked to deed and mortgage records, and asks how the price contrast changes as company buyers and possible related-party transfers are separated.

Key results

  • 9.25log points: financed house purchases above cash purchases of the same house (95% interval 7.17–11.35)
  • 4.87log points after excluding the 358 pairs with a same-surname deed, a marker of possible transfers between relatives (2.69–6.76)
  • 1.53log points when the cash buyer is a company; the interval (−1.79 to 5.44) includes zero

Public version 3.1. These are conditional associations from record linkage, not causal effects of financing.

Why it matters

  • A cash discount is often read as the value of closing certainty, and proposals to tax cash purchases—including one considered in New York in May 2026—treat it as the cash buyer's advantage. The measured gap depends heavily on who pays cash, so it is a poor basis for setting a tax or subsidy on the payment method.
  • Little of the gap belongs to the company buyers most often described as outbidding households.
  • Any study that uses administrative "cash" labels inherits transfers between relatives unless it screens for them.

Validation status

A frozen queue of 53 repeat-sale pairs (106 deed and mortgage endpoints; 40 drawn at random, 13 targeted diagnostics) still needs two independent, blinded human readings. The review will measure label error with uncertainty and show whether corrected classifications change the estimates. Until then the deed and financing classifications are a material limitation, and the estimates above are provisional on those labels.
