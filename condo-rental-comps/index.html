What RPTL § 581 actually does

Unlike one- to three-family Class 1 homes — which NYC Department of Finance (DOF) values close to recent market sales — Tax Class 2 condominiums and cooperatives are governed by New York Real Property Tax Law § 581. Under § 581, DOF must value the building as though it were a comparable rental apartment building, capitalizing net rental income from selected rental comparables printed on each Notice of Property Value (NOPV), and then apportioning that whole-building figure across units.

Linking all 968,294 FY2027 roll rows and the July 24 supplemental surcharge roll to 26,317 matched condominium sales and 36,352 Class 1 house sales (Working Paper v1.6), median DOF market values sit at 23.4% of sale price for condos versus 105.5% for houses.

What this means if you are preparing a Class 2 Form TC107 appeal

Because Phase 1 assesses condos at RPTL § 581 values through June 2028, submitting Zillow or ACRIS apartment sale comps alone does not refute DOF’s Class 2 valuation formula. In a Class 2 valuation review before March 1, 2027:

  1. Check primary-residence & long-term lease exemptions first (Form TC107 Step-by-Step Guide) — an exempt unit owes $0 regardless of DOF’s rental model.
  2. Pull your building’s NOPV rental-income comps & capitalization sheet: Qualified tax-certiorari counsel evaluate the rental comparables, gross income, and expense assumptions DOF used to set the building’s § 581 value, alongside unit-allocation shares.
